# Deck: Essential Daily Words (Sample)

---

### Card 1
* **Front (Kana):** こんにちは
* **Front (Romaji):** konnichiwa
* **Back (English):** Hello / Good afternoon

{?}
* **Context / Usage Note:** Standard daytime greeting (used roughly from 10:00 AM until dusk). Do not use this when greeting close family or very close friends at home; it sounds too distant or formal with them.
* **Example 1:**
  * **Kana:** こんにちは、おげんきですか？
  * **Romaji:** konnichiwa, ogenki desu ka?
  * **English:** Hello, how are you?
* **Example 2:**
  * **Kana:** たなかさん、こんにちは。
  * **Romaji:** tanaka-san, konnichiwa.
  * **English:** Hello, Mr./Ms. Tanaka.
* **Example 3:**
  * **Kana:** こんにちは、きょうはいいおてんきですね。
  * **Romaji:** konnichiwa, kyou wa ii otenki desu ne.
  * **English:** Hello, nice weather today, isn't it?

### Card 2
* **Front (Kana):** すみません
* **Front (Romaji):** sumimasen
* **Back (English):** Excuse me / I'm sorry / Thank you

{?}
* **Context / Usage Note:** One of the most versatile words in Japanese. It works in three major ways: to lightly apologize ("sorry"), to grab someone's attention (like calling a waiter), and as a humble "thank you" when someone goes out of their way to help you.
* **Example 1:**
  * **Kana:** すみません、これをおねがいします。
  * **Romaji:** sumimasen, kore o onegaishimasu.
  * **English:** Excuse me, this one please.
* **Example 2:**
  * **Kana:** おそくなって、すみません。
  * **Romaji:** osoku natte, sumimasen.
  * **English:** I'm sorry for being late.
* **Example 3:**
  * **Kana:** すみません、えきはどこですか？
  * **Romaji:** sumimasen, eki wa doko desu ka?
  * **English:** Excuse me, where is the station?

### Card 3
* **Front (Kana):** いちご
* **Front (Romaji):** ichigo
* **Back (English):** Strawberry

{?}
* **Context / Usage Note:** Straightforward noun (strawberry). Notice how the particle を (o) is used when you eat or buy it, and が (ga) when describing preference.
* **Example 1:**
  * **Kana:** あまいいちごをたべました。
  * **Romaji:** amai ichigo o tabemashita.
  * **English:** I ate a sweet strawberry.
* **Example 2:**
  * **Kana:** わたしはいちごがすきです。
  * **Romaji:** watashi wa ichigo ga suki desu.
  * **English:** I like strawberries.
* **Example 3:**
  * **Kana:** このいちごはいくらですか？
  * **Romaji:** kono ichigo wa ikura desu ka?
  * **English:** How much is this strawberry?
